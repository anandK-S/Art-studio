Atelier Philosophy

Preserving The Integrity of Hands

In an age of rapid mechanical replication, The Art Studio is committed to the quiet devotion of hand-tooling, natural binding agents, and ancestral craft lineages.

01

400-Year Heritage Lineage

Every Thikri mirror artisan and Madhubani master in our atelier descends from generational guild families, upholding techniques unchanged since royal Mughal and Rajput courts.

02

Over 140 Hours Per Work

From individual diamond-wheel glass scoring to the meticulous placement of micro-tesserae, our artworks undergo weeks of patient handcrafting and thermal curing.

03

Archival & Museum Durability

We utilize anti-tarnish protective silvering, kiln-annealed glasses, seasoned sustainable hardwoods, and museum-grade UV non-glare glazing for centuries of beauty.
